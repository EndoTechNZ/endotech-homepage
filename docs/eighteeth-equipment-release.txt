The homepage hero and carousel remain unchanged. The equipment section follows the existing product directory. Individual pages link to one another, manuals, manufacturer brochures, and training. Files and equipment share one pro forma request, with equipment pricing shown separately. A submitted request is not an order until confirmed by EndoTech NZ.

Training source: EndoTech's Eighteeth resource library. MP4 files were remuxed with stream copy and faststart. Original Cardinali 1920x1080 H.264 video and AAC audio are preserved. The files are stored in the eighteeth-training-20261015 GitHub release, fetched and verified by scripts/fetch-equipment-media.mjs at build time, and served from the site's normal /videos/eighteeth/ paths. SHA-256 and byte counts are pinned in shared/eighteeth-training-assets.json.

E-Connect S manual: supplied manufacturer IFU, Version 10, 31 March 2025.
Ultra X manual: manufacturer IFU-6235001, Version 11, 2 April 2025, 44 pages, sourced from https://abmed.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Ultra-X-User-Manual.pdf . Match the IFU revision to the device supplied.
Ultra X brochure: https://www.eighteeth.com/upload/2026/0723/UltraX.pdf
Ultra X tip/wrench instruction video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FRWDNrR0sq8 (Eighteeth Sifary).
