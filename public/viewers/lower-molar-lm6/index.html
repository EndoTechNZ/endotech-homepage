A Retrospective Clinical Study of Incidence of Root Canal Instrument Separation Iqbal, Kohli & Kim (2006)
Study findings

Where were rotary files recorded to separate?

Iqbal, Kohli and Kim’s retrospective study reviewed 4,865 endodontic cases. Among the reported rotary-file separations in molars:

  • Upper (maxillary) molars — mesiobuccal (MB) 70%, distobuccal (DB) 26%, palatal 4%.
  • Lower (mandibular) molars — mesiobuccal (MB) 61%, mesiolingual (ML) 24%, distal (D) 15%.

Mesiobuccal canals accounted for the largest share in both molar groups.
