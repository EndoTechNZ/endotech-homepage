Source and preparation method

← Complete molar collection

12 complete human molars, 29 roots and 40 canals with 41 preparation courses. Crown, chamber, roots, native masks and source files are preserved. The 72 µm native data cannot recover every fine terminal passage or histological constriction.

Illustrative apical completion

Stephen authorised separately labelled apical completions on 19 October 2026, with normal canal anatomy retained as the constraint. The native scans are unchanged. Terminal extensions follow each existing course, taper with noncircular sections and blend into it; they do not introduce extra branches or joins. Individual extensions, targets, controls and native evidence are recorded with each canal. Some reconstructed terminal courses span several millimetres. Further native apical fragments guide the continuation where present; intervening gaps and the selected internal landmarks remain illustrative. These regions must not be interpreted as observed specimen anatomy.

The model uses an explicitly selected internal teaching endpoint just inside a selected illustrative external portal. It is not a recovered minor foramen. Targets are measured along the curved passage, generally 0.15 mm inside the portal and 0.28 mm for ACTA 0002 Root A canal 2. These are model-specific authoring choices, not clinical working-length guidance. Every offered file in a canal reaches that same point.

ACTA0115 MB2 also has a separately recorded illustrative coronal connection, based on the user’s approach annotations. See the sizing and entry review for its source limitation.

Files and motion

Existing MicroPath 17/.03 and ET .04 source assemblies are reused without altering their source vertices. ET25 is the previously approved solid artwork; ET20, ET30 and ET35 are the existing explicitly nominal teaching derivatives, not size-specific certified manufacturer CAD. No larger ET size has been invented. Each canal offers a reviewed prefix of the available sequence, finishing at 25, 30 or 35. See the canal-by-canal sizing review and research. ACTA 0002 Root A canal 2 stops at ET25 because the sampled ET30/35 envelopes crossed the outer root at the selected target.

Motion uses a smooth fixed route with axial rotation applied before bending. The protected shank and handle remain a rigid assembly. Broad free spaces do not force a sharp deviation. Short voxel-scale blips are suppressed using Stephen’s approximate 0.5 mm modelling preference; this is not a validated NiTi mechanics law. The construction does not solve material forces, fatigue or clinical transportation.

The rotational geometric cutting envelope uses source-derived active profiles from 0.05 mm behind the distal tip through the recorded active length. The small distal cap and the protected assembly do not cut. This revision’s declared teaching interval follows inspection of the saved source-tip detail; it is not a manufacturer certification of cutting behaviour. Its evidence is retained in Research/CUTTING-INTERVAL-REVIEW.json.

Visible preparation and quantities

Persistent ISO wall colour identifies the stage that actually shapes a surface. Untouched native recesses stay coral. Withdrawal does not restore removed material. Switching canals preserves completed preparation. A matched section shows original canal, dentine and actual modelled additions at the same coordinates.

Removal is calculated from the cumulative visited cutter envelope against the initial teaching hard tissue, including separately authored apical passages. Per-file additional stock is sampled at 0.025 mm and whole-tooth totals use a union of aligned voxel IDs to avoid double counting overlapping canals. The displayed time is compressed teaching playback, 16 seconds per file. Geometric contact/removal is not evidence of clinical cleaning or disinfection.

Review and saved files

Each canal has route, target, source and instrument hashes, native oblique evidence, sampled protected-cap and outer-root checks. Final prepared tooth solids are closed. The review includes exact 10° exterior and canal-relation rotations for every root, exit-facing views and cutaways. Numeric checks do not certify clinical anatomy. Remaining fine source branches and constrictions stay unresolved.

Each tooth folder includes an editable Blender final-geometry scene, the complete source file-motion inspection rig, separate stock meshes, native-derived fields and its viewer. The Blender final wall geometry is static inspection geometry; the browser is the time-resolved progressive preparation viewer.
