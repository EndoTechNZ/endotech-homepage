TransformX™ PT is designed for clinicians who use a ProTaper-style progressive shaping workflow and want to preserve familiar sequence logic while improving control through demanding anatomy.

## Clinical Problem

Progressive taper systems need to shape efficiently from coronal access through apical finishing. The clinical risk is losing control when curvature, resistance, or incomplete glide path preparation places too much stress on the active file.

## TransformX™ Solution

TransformX™ PT keeps the recognisable SX, S1, S2, and F-file sequence while adding the control benefits of Transform Technology and Avatar Tip.

| Phase | Clinical objective | Control priority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coronal shaping | Create early space and improve access. | Avoid forcing SX apically. |
| Middle third shaping | Progress from coronal access toward working length. | Irrigate and recapitulate between files. |
| Apical finishing | Select final size based on anatomy and gauging. | Maintain centred advancement and working-length control. |

## Prerequisites

- Access cavity established.
- Working length determined.
- Patency confirmed.
- Glide path confirmed to working length before rotary shaping.

## Motor Settings

| Step | File | Speed | Torque | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | SX | 300 RPM | 3.0 Ncm | Coronal shaping |
| 2 | S1, S2 | 300 RPM | 3.0 Ncm | Middle third shaping |
| 3 | F1, F2, F3 | 300 RPM | 3.0 Ncm | Apical finishing |

## Step-by-Step Sequence

### Step 1: Coronal Shaping

- Use **SX** at 300 RPM, 3.0 Ncm.
- Create initial coronal flare.
- Establish straighter access to the mid-root.
- Irrigate thoroughly before moving deeper.

### Step 2: Middle Third Shaping

- Use **S1** at 300 RPM, 3.0 Ncm and shape to resistance.
- Irrigate, recapitulate, and re-irrigate.
- Use **S2** at 300 RPM, 3.0 Ncm and progress to working length when anatomy permits.
- Irrigate, recapitulate, and re-irrigate again.

### Step 3: Apical Finishing

- Use **F1** at 300 RPM, 3.0 Ncm to working length.
- Select **F2** or **F3** based on apical anatomy, gauging, and final preparation objective.
- Complete apical sizing without forcing the file beyond the prepared path.

## Key Features

| File | Tip size | Taper | Use case |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SX | 19 | Progressive | Coronal flaring |
| S1 | 17 | Progressive | Initial shaping |
| S2 | 20 | Progressive | Full-length shaping |
| F1 | 20 | .07 | Small canals |
| F2 | 25 | .08 | Medium canals |
| F3 | 30 | .09 | Larger canals |

## Clinical Benefits

- Preserves familiar progressive taper sequence logic.
- Supports safer advancement through curvature when glide path is controlled.
- Helps maintain centred preparation through the apical third.
- Reduces abrupt wall engagement by pairing sequence discipline with Avatar Tip guidance.
- Keeps motor settings simple for clinicians already using PT-style systems.
