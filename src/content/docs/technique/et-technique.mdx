TransformX™ ET is designed for clinicians familiar with EdgeTaper, EdgeX7, Race Evo, and other .04/.06 taper workflows. The aim is to preserve a familiar sequence while adding more controlled behaviour in curved and restricted anatomy.

## Clinical Problem

ET-style shaping is efficient when the glide path is stable and the file can progress without excessive lateral stress. Risk increases when the clinician asks the shaping file to negotiate anatomy that has not been prepared predictably.

## TransformX™ Solution

TransformX™ ET places glide path confirmation first, then uses a simple shaping and finishing sequence supported by Transform Technology and Avatar Tip.

| Phase | Clinical objective | Control priority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Glide path | Establish a reproducible path to working length. | Reduce negotiation load before shaping. |
| Shaping | Develop preparation with ET 20/.04. | Maintain light pressure and irrigation discipline. |
| Finishing | Complete apical sizing with ET 25/.04 or larger where indicated. | Preserve working length and canal centering. |

## Prerequisites

- Access cavity established.
- Working length determined.
- Patency confirmed.
- Glide path confirmed before rotary shaping.

## Motor Settings

| Step | File | Speed | Torque | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Acrobat 13/.03 | 300 RPM | 2.0-2.5 Ncm | Glide path |
| 2 | ET 20/.04 | 350 RPM | 3.0 Ncm | Shaping |
| 3 | ET 25/.04 | 350 RPM | 3.0 Ncm | Finishing |

:::tip[Speed range]
ET files can run at speeds up to **500 RPM** if preferred. Use clinical judgement based on canal anatomy, access, tactile feedback, and resistance.
:::

## Step-by-Step Sequence

### Step 1: Glide Path

- Use **Acrobat 13/.03** at 300 RPM, 2.0-2.5 Ncm.
- Establish a reproducible glide path to working length.
- Irrigate thoroughly before shaping.

### Step 2: Shaping

- Use **ET 20/.04** at 350 RPM, 3.0 Ncm.
- Shape to working length with light apical pressure.
- Irrigate, recapitulate, and re-irrigate.

### Step 3: Finishing

- Use **ET 25/.04** at 350 RPM, 3.0 Ncm.
- Complete apical sizing.
- Select larger sizes such as 30/.04 or 35/.04 when anatomy and gauging support a larger final preparation.

## Key Features

- Familiar ET-style .04/.06 workflow.
- Acrobat glide path integration before shaping.
- Transform Technology for adaptive file response through changing canal demands.
- Avatar Tip for more controlled apical guidance.
- Simple motor settings that fit common rotary protocols.

## Clinical Benefits

- Cleaner transition from glide path to shaping.
- More predictable progression through curved anatomy.
- Better apical control when working length and irrigation are maintained.
- Reduced tendency to force the shaping file through unprepared anatomy.
- Familiar workflow with improved control, safety, and adaptability.
