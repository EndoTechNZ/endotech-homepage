TransformX™ is designed to preserve familiar rotary technique while adding more controlled shaping behaviour. Use these settings as a practical reference, then adapt within the clinician's usual judgement for anatomy, access, irrigation, and file feedback.

## Quick Reference

| System | Speed | Torque | Clinical role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Acrobat Glide Path | 300 RPM | 2.0-2.5 Ncm | Establish and confirm a reproducible glide path before shaping. |
| TransformX™ ET | 350 RPM | 3.0 Ncm | Shape and finish ET-style .04/.06 taper preparations. |
| TransformX™ PT | 300 RPM | 3.0 Ncm | Preserve progressive PT-style coronal-to-apical sequence logic. |

:::tip[Clinical control]
Motor settings support the sequence, but file behaviour still depends on access, glide path quality, irrigation, and resistance. If the file binds, withdraw, irrigate, recapitulate, and re-enter without apical pressure.
:::

## TransformX™ ET Settings

For clinicians using EdgeTaper, EdgeX7, Race Evo, or similar .04/.06 taper systems.

| Step | File | Speed | Torque | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Acrobat 13/.03 | 300 RPM | 2.0-2.5 Ncm | Glide path |
| 2 | ET 20/.04 | 350 RPM | 3.0 Ncm | Shaping |
| 3 | ET 25/.04 | 350 RPM | 3.0 Ncm | Finishing |

ET files can run at speeds up to **500 RPM** if preferred. Use the lower end when tactile feedback is more important, especially in restricted or curved anatomy.

### ET Sequence Summary

1. Establish the glide path with Acrobat to working length.
2. Shape with ET 20/.04 to working length.
3. Finish with ET 25/.04, or select larger finishing sizes when anatomy supports it.

Between every file: irrigate, recapitulate, and re-irrigate.

## TransformX™ PT Settings

For clinicians using ProTaper Gold, ProTaper Next, or similar progressive taper systems.

| Step | File | Speed | Torque | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | SX | 300 RPM | 3.0 Ncm | Coronal shaping |
| 2 | S1, S2 | 300 RPM | 3.0 Ncm | Middle third shaping |
| 3 | F1, F2, F3 | 300 RPM | 3.0 Ncm | Apical finishing |

### PT Sequence Summary

1. Use SX for controlled coronal flaring.
2. Shape with S1 and S2, progressing carefully toward working length.
3. Finish with F1, F2, or F3 according to apical anatomy and gauging.

Between every file: irrigate, recapitulate, and re-irrigate.

## General Guidelines

### Speed Settings

- Standard TransformX™ rotary use sits within familiar **300-500 RPM** ranges.
- Lower speeds can increase tactile feedback in demanding anatomy.
- Higher speeds may improve cutting efficiency where glide path and irrigation are controlled.

### Torque Settings

- Recommended torque range is generally **2.0-3.0 Ncm**, depending on file size and stage.
- Auto-reverse or auto-stop torque control is recommended.
- Treat motor feedback as clinical information; binding usually means the canal needs irrigation, recapitulation, or a lighter touch.

### Key Principles

1. Confirm glide path and patency before rotary shaping.
2. Let the file advance without excessive apical pressure.
3. Irrigate frequently, especially between sequence steps.
4. Recapitulate with a small hand file to maintain patency.
5. Inspect files and discard any showing fatigue or deformation.
