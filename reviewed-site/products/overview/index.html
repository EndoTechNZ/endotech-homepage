Rotary shaping systems

Transform S™ ET, PT and RG form the core shaping pathways.

Rotary shaping system

Transform S™ ET

ET-style shaping for clinicians who want familiar .04/.06 sequence logic and a clear apical sequence, positioned as a replacement for EdgeFile X7, HyFlex CM, FKG RACE EVO / RaCe, MicroMega, Komet FQ, and HERO systems.

Rotary shaping system

Transform S™ PT

PT-style shaping for clinicians preserving progressive crown-down sequence logic through more demanding anatomy, positioned as a replacement for ProTaper, ProTaper Gold, and ProTaper Next.

Reciprocating shaping system

Transform S™ RG

Reciprocating shaping with a dedicated Glider and Small, Primary, Medium and Large options for clinicians using WaveOne Gold-style workflows.
