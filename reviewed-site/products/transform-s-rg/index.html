A clear reciprocating pathway.

1

Assess and open

Preserve conservative access objectives while creating a pathway that still respects the natural canal form.

2

Glide and shape

Establish the path with the dedicated RG Glider, then select the shaping size that fits the anatomy and preparation objective.

3

Clean and obturate

Move from reciprocating shaping into irrigation and a matched sealing pathway so preparation supports the next clinical step.
