Mechanism View

Adaptability and guidance belong together.

Curved canals ask the file to cut, flex, and follow the prepared path at the same time. The technology platform separates those demands into two design controls so the clinical sequence stays familiar.

  • Coronal stability supports efficient shaping and torsional control.
  • Apical adaptability helps reduce unnecessary restoring-force pressure.
  • Rounded guidance geometry supports smoother progression near working length.

Product Connection

The technology leads back to clinical sequence selection.

Once the clinician understands the control platform, the practical question becomes sequence preference. Transform S™ ET and Transform S™ PT preserve familiar shaping pathways while using the same technology logic.

Transform S™ ET ET-style sequence logic for clinicians who want familiar .04 and .06 shaping behaviour with the Transform S control platform. Transform S™ PT PT-style progressive shaping sequence using the same material response and apical guidance platform.
